# POV Rajput 0.3.0

## New: POV Rajput for phones

POV Rajput now comes as two apps from one codebase. Download the one for your device:

- **`POV-Rajput-TV.apk`** — Android TV / Google TV. Same app as before; existing TV installs update in place. (`POV-Rajput-release.apk` is the same file under its old name.)
- **`POV-Rajput-Mobile.apk`** — phones. A separate app (it can sit beside the TV app), with its own settings and updates.

The phone app has every feature of the TV app with a layout made for touch:

- Portrait screens with a **bottom navigation bar** (Home, Movies, Shows, Anime, Library, Search, WeTrakr, Settings).
- Home banner with title logo, Play / More Info / My List, Top 10 rows and shelves; two-column grids.
- **Touch player** in landscape: tap to show or hide the controls, double-tap left/right to skip 10 s (middle: play/pause), drag sideways to scrub, drag up/down on the left for brightness and on the right for volume.
- Search with the keyboard open straight away; Back closes it.
- Settings rows wrap long values under their label; a mini player sits above the navigation bar while you browse.

## Anime

- The details page shows the title's own picture at once (never the previous title's), and loads backdrops about four times smaller, so they appear sooner.
- **Play comes right under the synopsis**, followed by one row of related entries: Seasons, Movies, Specials and Spin-offs.
- Long synopses are shortened with a **More** button, and Kitsu credit notes such as "(Source: …)" are removed.
- Reopening a title within 10 minutes shows its episodes and related entries instantly.
- The grid fills the full width, and on TV **Search anime** is the first button.

## Also

- **Check for updates on startup** is now on by default (it stays off if you turned it off).
- TV search opens with the keyboard closed: press OK on the field to type, or go straight to Voice search.
- The synopsis window no longer squeezes its Close button when the text is long.
