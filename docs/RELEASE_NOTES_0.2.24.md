# POV Rajput 0.2.24

## Your language first

- **Links in your audio language are listed and auto-played first.** Set Audio language (and Subtitle language) in Settings → Subtitles & audio. Links tagged with your language come first, then untagged links when the title's original language is yours, then multi-audio links, then the rest. Quality and size sorting still apply inside each group. Turn it off under Sort → Language.
- **Reads provider language lines.** Flag lines such as Meteor's `🌐` (audio) and `💬` (subtitles) and Torrentio's flags are understood, as well as names like Hindi, Português, PT-BR, Dublado, Latino, VOSTFR and Legendado.
- **English fallback** (on by default). If a video has no audio or subtitles in your language, the player picks English instead of a random track, and English links are listed before other languages.
- Matching links show a language, MULTI AUDIO or SUBS badge.
- Source sorting is much faster on long result lists.

## Player

- **Now-playing badges** between the seek and Audio buttons: quality (4K, 1080p…), file size, source (WEB-DL, BLU-RAY, REMUX…), codec, DV / HDR10+ / HDR10 and audio (ATMOS, DD+, DTS-HD MA… and 5.1 / 7.1). Quality, codec, HDR and audio come from what is actually being decoded.
- **Live subtitle sync.** Subtitles → Subtitle sync opens a small panel at the top: Left/Right move subtitles 0.1 s earlier/later while you watch, with Reset to 0. Applies to the current video.
- The title logo now also shows while the player controls are open, and hides with them.

## Fixes

- Changing the audio or subtitle language in Settings no longer jumps back to the top of the page.
